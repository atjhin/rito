Inside the Last Drop, Vander, now a bartender, settles a trade dispute when he sees the kids returning injured. He scolds them for the damage caused by their failed heist, but Vi defends their actions. Vander reminds her that she needs to lead more responsibly and that violence will only cause more problems, to which she reluctantly agrees. He tells her they need to lay low for a while and that he'll take care things. Vander leaves for Benzo's Pawn Shop with Claggor. Powder realizes she still has blue crystals and races to show the others, but overhears Vi and Mylo talking about her. Mylo rants about Powder's shortcomings and Vi seemingly agrees with him, causing Powder to run off, but in truth she defends her and retorts that Mylo has his own issues, such as not knowing how to keep his mouth shut.
