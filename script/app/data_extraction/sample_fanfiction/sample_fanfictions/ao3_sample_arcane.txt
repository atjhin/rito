Jayce opened his eyes as the explosion died down and he looked at himself to see that he was unharmed, causing him to look around in confusion before seeing a magical barrier shielding him from the destruction. Jayce looked at the shield in bewilderment before looking in front of him and saw a sight that stunned him with disbelief as Asmund stood in front of him covered in the same shield, holding the crystal as his eyes glowed red and blue lightning aura covered his body. Asmund held out an outstretched hand while a glowing runic symbol hovered in the air in front of him just like the symbol on the board and Jayce’s eyes widened in shock at the sight.

“Looks like I know what the rune means now.” Asmund commented as he looked at the rune floating in front of him, “ Shield .”

“How…?” Jayce questioned as he stared at Asmund in awe and bewilderment as the shields disappeared and Asmund’s aura vanished while his eyes returned to normal, “This changes everything .”

Asmund heard a commotion down below in the streets and he could only hope that the kids had enough smarts to get back to Zaun while more Enforcers came upstairs as Asmund handed Jayce the crystals, just as the Enforcers barged into the room and aimed their guns at Asmund who glared at them defiantly.

“Don’t kill him!” Jayce pleaded with the Enforcers before turning to Asmund, “Please surrender.”

Asmund slanted his eyes darkly as he analyzed his situation, knowing that he was outnumbered even if he unleashed the beast from within so he raised his hands in surrender as he glanced out the window.

“Take him.” one of the Enforcers ordered another and two of the Enforcers came up to Asmund and handcuffed him before taking him away.

Asmund glanced at Jayce as he was pushed by the other man and taken out of the apartment and into a vehicle to transport him to the jail where he would await a trial, only hoping that Vi and the others got home safely.
